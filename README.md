# Somewhere to Here

A compass back to the places you leave: the car, the festival tent, the meeting spot.
For the Nothing Phone (3), in the same family as Somewhere to Nothing, Somewhere to my Someone
and Somewhere Up There.

- **Three pins.** Each has its own shape so you can tell them apart at a glance: a solid round
  head, a hollow square, a hollow triangle. Tap a pin to give it a name ("CAR") and a note
  ("LEVEL 2 · BAY 41").
- **Pinning waits for a good fix.** It saves the spot by itself once the phone is sure to about
  10 metres, or you can press PIN ANYWAY.
- **NEAR.** When you're closer than GPS can tell apart, the arrow goes away and it says NEAR,
  so it never points you the wrong way in the last few metres. Then: FOUND IT.
- **Widget** (2×2 and 4×2): the pin you last tapped, with distance and walking time. North is up.
  With no pins, it has a PIN THIS SPOT button.
- **Glyph Toy:** a little map of your pins, with you in the middle. Long-press the Glyph Button
  to step through the map and each pin.
- **Private:** no account and no internet permission. Your pins never leave the phone.

## Install

Open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) on the phone and tap
**somewhere-to-here.apk**, then open it to install. New versions install over the old one and keep
your pins.
